ORIGINAL PLUGIN

Hermes Pixel Office was originally created by Teknium — a pixel-art virtual office that turns every Hermes agent into an animated character at a desk. This fork, Hermes Pixel Office Enhanced, builds on that clean foundation and takes it further.

teknium1/hermes-pixel-office
THE ENHANCEMENT — BY DRGEKOZ

I'm DrGekoz. I made Hermes IdleViber, a cozy pixel idle game. When the office came along, I started adapting the bits of IdleViber I love most — its tier ladder, all 500 pixel icons, and its peer-to-peer mesh — into the plugin. So the office became a place you can actually live in: a GitHub leaderboard, a 500-rung tier ladder, office chat, and custom profiles and themes, all shared with friends over P2P.
